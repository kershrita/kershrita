### 👋 About me

I'm an **AI Engineer** at **Adwat Information Technology** (Cairo), where I design, build and ship ML features behind secure APIs, from recommendation models to LLM-powered services. I hold a **B.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence** (GPA 3.52/4) and I'm certified by **Microsoft Azure** and **NVIDIA** in LLMs and deep learning.

- 🔭 **Working on:** production ML & LLM systems: RAG, AI agents, evaluation and fine-tuning
- 🌱 **Learning:** multi-GPU training and LLM customization
- 🎓 **Former** Microsoft Learn Student Ambassador (campus lead); 🥈 2nd place in the Ambassadors AI Project with *Parasite Hunter*
- 🤖 **Also into:** edge AI and embedded systems (ESP32, Arduino, TinyML)
